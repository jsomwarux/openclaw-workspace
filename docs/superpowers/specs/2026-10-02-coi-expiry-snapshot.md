# COI Expiry Snapshot

**Status:** Pre-registered fallback only. It does not run unless the first 25 sends and standard follow-up cadence produce zero positive replies, the reply-path test passed, bounce rate remains under the ceiling, complaints remain zero, and JT separately approves it.

## Input

- The contractor's current active-subcontractor list and certificates of insurance.
- Maximum 10 certificates; return additional certificates unread.
- Optional insurance requirements for one job.
- The contractor supplies the files. JT does not enter their systems or contact subcontractors or brokers.

## Output

Within 48 hours, deliver one page listing:

1. Certificates already expired or expiring within 30 days, with the date.
2. Subcontractors with no named owner for renewal follow-up.
3. Certificate-to-job-requirement mismatches only when the contractor supplied those requirements.

Use plain language with no scores or grades. The delivery ask is the $1,500 audit.

## Scope boundary

- Report only what each certificate states and who owns follow-up.
- Never opine on coverage adequacy, interpret policy terms, or declare a subcontractor compliant.
- Every page states: "This page reports what the certificates state and who follows up. It is not insurance or legal advice."

## Deletion rule

- Delete every certificate and the subcontractor list within 24 hours after delivery, including attachments and working copies.
- Retain only the delivery date in the outcome record—no names or certificate data.

## Limits

- Maximum five snapshots.
- Budget about two hours each.
- Only the front end changes for the next 25 sends; list, copy structure, and sender remain fixed.
