## 2. New factual input: competitive research

Web research conducted August 2026. These are findings, not interpretation.
Several figures are unofficial or third-party reported and are marked.

### Leasing / resident communication AI

- **EliseAI** — NYC-based. ~$2.2B valuation, ~$392M raised, ~599 employees. Used
  by 600+ operators including 38 of the NMHC Top 50. Covers leasing, resident
  messaging, tours, maintenance requests, renewals, delinquency. Reported pricing
  ~$3–6 per unit per month with a ~$25,000 annual minimum *(unofficial)*.
- **Colleen AI** acquired by Entrata (2024). **Knock CRM** acquired by RealPage (2022).
- Smaller players: Zuma, LetHub, Haven, Vellum, Leasey.ai, RentEngine, Super.

### NYC building compliance monitoring

- **SiteCompli** — mature NYC incumbent. Its InCheck product scans DOB, FDNY,
  HPD, DEP, DOT databases for new violations and alerts managers; aggregates
  compliance across hundreds of buildings. Used by large managers including
  FirstService Residential. Priced per building, aimed at large portfolios.
- Others: **KomplyOS**, **Insparisk (Command)**, **BCompliant**, **Propmaticly**,
  **ViolationWatch**, **BCM New York**. **UpCodes** for code research.

### COI / vendor insurance tracking

- **Jones** — NYC-based, real estate and construction focused. Customers include
  Rudin Management and Prologis.
- Others: **myCOI**, **TrustLayer**, **Certificial**, **SmartCompliance**, **bcs**.
- **NetVendor** — vendor lifecycle, supports Yardi, RealPage, Entrata, AppFolio,
  MRI, ResMan and Rent Manager concurrently. Reports customer savings around
  $30,000/year and 30 hours/week.

### Native PMS AI

AppFolio (**Realm-X**), Yardi, Buildium, RealPage and Entrata all now embed AI
across leasing, maintenance, communications and reporting.

### Subsidy / affordable housing recertification

Initially hypothesized as an uncontested category. **That was checked and found
to be wrong.**

- **Yardi Voyager** — Certify/Certs module, automated Section 8 recertifications,
  HUD form generation, audit-ready reporting.
- **RealPage** — Affordable Compliance module: initial certifications, annual
  recerts, interim adjustments, TRACS reporting.
- **Entrata**, **MRI**, **AppFolio** — affordable housing modules with
  recertification workflows and HUD EIV integration.
- **ExactEstate** — LIHTC, HUD Section 8 (project-based and tenant-based),
  Section 202/811; automated recert notices, TRACS submissions.
- **Axcces** — AI-native affordable housing platform; automates the full
  recertification cycle including reminders, document collection, eligibility
  verification and agency-ready certifications across HCV, PBV, LIHTC, Section 8.
- Reported enterprise pricing in this category: ~$2–5 per unit per month plus
  setup, or $5,000+/month for mid-sized portfolios *(third-party listings,
  unverified)*.

One distinction noted but **not verified**: these products are largely oriented
to federally subsidized properties (TRACS, 50059s, HUD EIV, LIHTC). Whether a
conventional NYC landlord with scattered HCV/CityFHEPS tenants has a materially
different and less-served workflow is an open question.

### Pricing benchmarks observed

- Yardi Breeze — from ~$1/unit/month
- AppFolio — from ~$250/month core
- SiteCompli — per building; roughly $100–500/month at small scale *(unofficial)*
- Industry convention is **per unit per month**.
