# The 90 Day Playbook
**Written 2026-08-19. Runs through 2026-11-17.**

---

## Part 1: What you sell, in one sentence

> You take one messy business process, build a controlled AI system around it, and leave behind proof the business can trust.

Say this exact thing. Do not add "any industry." Do not explain agents building agents. Nobody buys that. They buy the sentence above.

**Who you say it to first:** NYC and NJ property managers and building owners.

**Why property first:** you already know the words, you already know people, and you already have proof. This is not a permanent decision. It is the fastest door.

**The parallel rule:** if someone outside property comes to you through your network, take the call and quote it. Do not build a research system to find them. Just answer the phone.

---

## Part 2: The one number you track

**Priced conversations per week. Target: 2.**

A priced conversation means you told a real buyer a real number. Not a coffee chat. Not "let me know if you need anything." A number.

Two a week for twelve weeks is about 24 shots. That is what decides whether this quarter works.

Write the number on a sticky note. Check it Friday. That is the whole tracking system.

**The scoreboard number:** cash collected. Nothing else counts as progress.

---

## Part 3: The three things you sell

Sell them in this order. Each one leads to the next.

### Layer 1: The Workflow Audit
- **Price:** $1,500 flat
- **Time:** one week
- **What they get:** you map one process, find where money and hours leak, and tell them honestly whether to build or not build
- **The hook:** the $1,500 comes off the price if they hire you to build it

This is your foot in the door. It is much easier to say yes to $1,500 than $8,000. And it gets paid either way.

### Layer 2: The Build
- **Price:** $6,000 to $12,000 fixed
- **Time:** three to five weeks
- **Terms:** 50% up front, 50% on acceptance
- **What they get:** the working system, plus a written acceptance checklist so "done" is not an argument

### Layer 3: Managed AI Ops
- **Price:** $1,500 per month
- **What they get:** you watch it, handle exceptions, send a monthly accuracy report

This is the recurring money. It is also the closest thing to your long term goal. It only sells once a client has seen the monthly report exist. So the report has to exist.

### Layer 4 (later, not now): Partner Studio
Equity or revenue share where your system becomes the business. This is what the Altmark partnership is. Do not try to sell this to anyone else yet. One at a time.

## Part 5: Your week

Same every week. No thinking required.

**Monday morning: outreach.** Five touches. New asks plus follow-ups. Ninety minutes, then stop.

**Tuesday through Thursday: deliver.** Paid work only. This is where the billable hours go.

**Friday, 30 minutes: the scoreboard.** Write down three things.
1. Cash collected this week
2. Priced conversations this week
3. Proposals sitting out there waiting

Nothing else. No reorganizing, no new systems, no new ideas file.

**Four protected hours a week: the owned asset.** Right now that is Watchdog, and those hours go to selling it, not polishing it.

**Frozen:**

You are not killing these. You are parking them. Write the list somewhere and stop looking at it.

## Part 7: The Altmark decision

This is the biggest single number on your board and it has been drifting since August 11.

**This week, send one short note.** Say something like: you are planning your capacity for the fall, and if there is no signed employment agreement and a NewCo term sheet by September 15, you will assume it is not happening this quarter and fill the time with client work.

Not a threat. A calendar.

**Before you send it, write down your walk away, for yourself:**
1. The lowest total package you will accept
2. What IP stays yours, in writing
3. Who owns what percentage of NewCo, in a number

If they cannot answer number 3 by the date, that is your answer.

**Keep the recruiter conversations warm.** Dino and the others cost you nothing and they are leverage.

## Part 8: The 90 day gate

On November 17, sit down and answer five questions with evidence, not feelings.

1. Which type of client paid fastest?
2. Which buyer understood you with the least explaining?
3. Which same problem showed up in more than one prospect?
4. Which piece of proof made the next sale easier?
5. Which one could support a $1,500 a month retainer?

Whatever the answers say, that is your niche. Not what a research system says. What the money says.
