## Required repairs

1. Keep one LinkedIn purpose, with three eligible source types:
   - Shipped implementation proof: what was breaking, what was built, where the human approves, how correctness was verified, and the outcome.
   - Current buyer/operator problem judgment: only a dated public signal already verified through Revenue Signal Lab, an existing client conversation, or completed sales research.    These are eligible source types, not three posting quotas.

2. Set a minimum-density floor of one strong post per week until the profile contains six to eight current, relevant posts. After that, silence is allowed when no source clears the gates. Allow a second post only for unusually strong shipped proof or a current buyer signal. Do not optimize for volume.

3. Replace the hard priority stop with a sequence gate. If a controllable priced follow-up is unsent, JT sends it first. Content may run afterward. Do not block content merely because buyers have not replied or the weekly conversation outcome has not yet occurred.

4. Keep property judgment on LinkedIn. Trade newsletters are secondary repurposing only after a real submission path or acceptance exists. They do not replace the channel JT controls.

6. Private/client screenshots remain banned. Permissioned public artifacts or deliberately rebuilt dummy artifacts may be used only when they materially prove the mechanism and pass the existing privacy checks.
