Each building’s super texts one dated meter photo a month.

When DEP posts a bill PDF, the workflow extracts the read type, meter index, service dates and usage. It flags estimates, checks them against the photo and prior history, then recalculates the charge.

If it’s wrong, the workflow puts a completed dispute packet and meter evidence into the owner’s approval queue, then tracks every appeal deadline. The owner reviews and submits it.

First run audits every estimated bill in the portfolio still inside DEP’s four-year dispute window.
