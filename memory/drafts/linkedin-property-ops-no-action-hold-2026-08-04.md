When the source report is missing, the system pauses the row.

That is the rule I would build into any AI layer touching property operations.

Tenant balance missing its report.
Lease date conflicts with the system of record.
Vendor follow-up waiting on owner approval.
Deposit chase with money attached and no confirmed next step.

Those rows need a visible hold.

The workflow shows the missing context, names the reviewer, keeps the source attached, and logs why the row stayed put.

That single rule protects the business from confident activity on incomplete context.

AI adoption gets safer when the system has permission to do less.
