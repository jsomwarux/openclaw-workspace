## Strategic Theme This Week

Controlled execution is the product.

The reusable asset is a source-to-decision trail that can answer:

- which source record started the workflow
- what the workflow was allowed to draft, reconcile, summarize, or prepare
- which tenant, resident, owner, lease, money, compliance, or client-sensitive action was held
- who had approval authority
- which tool, agent, or system boundary applied
- why the action moved, stopped, or escalated
- where the proof, audit path, and next cash step live

This maps directly to Altmark delinquency, DHCR renewal/deposit, MSI delivery governance, and the broader Run Control / AI Enablement OS pitch.
