# NYC DOB Inspections Plus — Property-Operations Workflow Signal

Date: 2026-09-16

## Verified signal

- NYC DOB moved Electrical Development Inspection requests into **DOB NOW: Inspections Plus** beginning June 29, 2026.
- Owners, licensed professionals, filing representatives, and delegates can request inspections/re-inspections, respond to objections, upload certification documents, manage delegates, cancel inspections, and request withdrawal inspections in the new module.
- The release currently covers Electrical Development Inspections, with additional work types expected later.

Sources:
- https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/dob/service-updates.page
- https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/industry/dob-now-inspectionplus.page
- https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/industry/dob-now-inspectionplus-faqs.page

## Buyer implication

Multi-building owners and property managers now have a second inspections surface whose records can fragment across buildings, permits, delegates, objections, and uploaded evidence. The immediate workflow-control opportunity is a portfolio inspection register that records:

1. building and permit identity;
2. responsible licensed professional/delegate;
3. inspection request and re-inspection state;
4. open objections and response deadlines;
5. evidence uploaded and certification status;
6. cancellation/withdrawal reason; and
7. final sign-off proof with an exception queue.

## Consulting angle

Offer a bounded **DOB inspection exception-and-evidence workflow** for a small portfolio: ingest an owner-provided export or tracker, normalize the state machine, surface aging objections and missing proof, and produce an auditable weekly exception report. Keep credentials and DOB submissions with the owner or licensed professional; the automation coordinates evidence and status only.

No outreach, Mission Control task, external send, credential, or schedule change was created from this research.
