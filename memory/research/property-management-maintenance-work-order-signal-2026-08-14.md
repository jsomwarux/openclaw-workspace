## Readout

The current property-management AI signal is converging on maintenance intake, triage, routing, and PMS work-order creation, not broad "AI for real estate" messaging.

Useful positioning for JT:
- Lead with a controlled maintenance/delinquency work queue, not an agent demo.
- Make the AppFolio/Yardi/Entrata/Rent Manager integration point explicit, then show the review gate: source request, urgency classification, allowed action, reviewer, PMS writeback state, and exception path.
- The wedge is strongest for small and mid-sized operators that already trust the PMS but do not have a governed control layer around what AI is allowed to create, update, or escalate.
- Avoid claiming full automation. The buyer-safe claim is: fewer missed requests, cleaner handoffs, and proof of what was routed, held, or escalated.
