## Run Control Translation

For Altmark/DHCR proof work, the useful control row is:

`client | workflow | source artifact | amount/deadline | required notice/rule | owner approval | send artifact | delivery proof | next cash event`

This keeps automation out of the sensitive decision and inside the evidence path:

- Automate extraction of source rows, aging, due amounts, lease dates, renewal windows, and artifact links.
- Hold tenant-impacting, legal/compliance, or money-sensitive sends behind named JT/client approval.
- Record final send state with delivery proof and a next cash event.
- Treat missing approval, missing source artifact, or uncertain timing as a block, not as copy polish.
