# NYC Property-Operations Signal — HPD Catch-up + LL33 Posting Window

Date: 2026-09-08
Status: verified buyer signal; no outreach sent

## Signal

NYC residential property operators are between two recurring compliance gates:

- HPD property registration was due September 1. It applies annually to multiple dwellings with 3+ residential units and qualifying 1–2 unit properties. A valid registration requires the annual fee and a properly completed registration statement. HPD also requires updates when ownership, managing-agent, or contact information changes.
- For buildings on the 2026 Covered Buildings List, DOB makes the annual Building Energy Efficiency Rating Label available October 1. Owners must download, print, and display it near every public entrance by October 31. Failure to display can result in a $1,250 violation.

## Buyer pain

The operational risk is not understanding either rule; it is proving portfolio-wide completion across changing owners, agents, addresses, entrances, exemptions, and exception cases. A spreadsheet or calendar reminder does not reliably capture:

1. which buildings are in scope;
2. registration/payment/form completion state;
3. label availability and retrieval;
4. posting at every public entrance;
5. photographic proof and exception correspondence;
6. accountable owner and escalation date.

## Consulting angle

Use this as a diagnostic conversation with NYC property managers and owner-operators:

> How are you proving, building by building, that September registrations are valid and every covered entrance gets its October energy label—with evidence ready if DOB challenges it?

A bounded pilot could create one portfolio control register, exception queue, owner/agent data check, evidence folder convention, and weekly completion dashboard. Position it as workflow control and audit readiness, not legal advice.

## Qualification questions

- How many NYC residential buildings and public entrances are managed?
- Is there one authoritative BBL/building roster with registration and LL33 scope?
- Who owns payment, form submission, label retrieval, physical posting, and proof capture?
- Where are photos, confirmations, exemptions, and failed-download correspondence stored?
- Which exception is hardest to see before it becomes a violation?
