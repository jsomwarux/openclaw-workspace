# NYC Short-Term Rental Registration Renewals — 2026-09-09

## Verified signal
- NYC's Office of Special Enforcement requires short-term-rental hosts to register, bars registrations for rent-regulated and NYCHA units, lets owners place buildings on a prohibited-buildings list, and requires booking services to verify registrations before processing transactions.
- Multiple current operator guides report that OSE expects the first registration renewals to open in October 2026 for registrations issued during the program's initial 2023 wave. Treat the October timing as an expected operational window until OSE publishes the final renewal instructions.

## Buyer/workflow implication
Property managers and compliant short-term-rental operators need one renewal control plane that inventories registrations, maps each listing to owner/building restrictions, tracks supporting evidence and renewal status, and blocks exception cases from reaching booking channels. The contained offer is a renewal-readiness audit plus an exception/evidence tracker—not legal advice and not a booking-platform replacement.
