# NYC HPD registration nonfiling — Council notice watch

Date: 2026-09-17
Status: proposed legislation; not current law

## Verified signal

NYC Council Introduction 1034-2026 was introduced on 2026-09-10 and referred to the Committee on Housing and Buildings. If enacted, HPD would have to send each Council Member, by October 1 annually, the addresses in that member's district whose owners failed to file required residential property-registration statements.

Source: https://legistar.council.nyc.gov/LegislationDetail.aspx?GUID=FC35973F-3FE1-4C84-B160-FEED1E077512&ID=8206776&Options=&Search=

HPD already requires qualifying residential properties to register annually. Registration is operationally important because an invalid registration can block violation certification/dismissal requests and other owner actions.

Source: https://www.nyc.gov/site/hpd/services-and-information/register-your-property.page

## Buyer implication

If the proposal advances, registration nonfiling would become more visible at the Council-district level, not merely an internal compliance defect. Multi-building owners and property managers would benefit from a portfolio control that tracks:

- registration status and renewal evidence by building;
- the $13 payment and completed HPD submission as separate completion gates;
- ownership/managing-agent changes that trigger an update;
- unresolved exceptions, owner, due date, and proof link;
- district mapping for buildings at risk of appearing on a nonfiler list.

Positioning angle: **prevent a routine registration exception from becoming a public or political escalation.**

## Boundary

This is a policy-watch signal, not a claim that the bill has passed. No outreach, Mission Control task, or schedule change was created.
