# NYC Non-Primary Residence Surcharge — Property-Ops Workflow Signal

**Date checked:** 2026-09-10  
**Primary source:** [NYC Department of Finance — Non-primary residence property surcharge](https://home4.nyc.gov/site/finance/property/non-primary-residence-surcharge.page)

## Verified signal

- NYC's new annual surcharge may apply in tax years 2026-27 and 2027-28 to one-to-three-family homes valued by DOF at $5M+ and co-op/condo units valued at $1M+.
- Owners who received a DOF notice and believe the property is exempt must submit an exemption application by **October 6, 2026**.
- Evidence varies by occupancy/ownership structure: primary-residence documents, lease and rental evidence for tenants, family-relationship records, or entity/governance documents and a majority-interest affidavit.
- DOF reviews submissions, issues a determination, and allows an appeal path through the NYC Tax Commission after denial.

## Buyer/workflow implication

For co-op/condo boards, family offices, and managers of high-value residential portfolios, this is a short-deadline evidence-orchestration problem: notice intake, property/unit matching, owner or occupant outreach, document completeness, deadline tracking, submission receipt, determination, and appeal routing. A contained diagnostic can map that exception queue and evidence chain across affected units without offering tax or legal advice.
