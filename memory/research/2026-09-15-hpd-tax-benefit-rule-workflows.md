# HPD tax-benefit rule workflows — 2026-09-15

## Verified signal

NYC HPD lists two adopted rule changes taking effect in September 2026:

- Effective **September 19, 2026**, qualifying 421-a(16) projects with a timely Notice of Intent may amend an approved workbook when changing from affordability option C or G to option A, B, E, or F.
- Effective **September 13, 2026**, 485-x homeownership projects may file for benefits once at least 50% of units are sold, provided proof of every remaining sale is supplied before benefits are granted.

Source: [NYC HPD Rules](https://www.nyc.gov/site/hpd/about/rules.page)

## Buyer/workflow implication

Affordable-housing developers, owners, and their compliance teams now have a concrete control problem across project, unit, and document levels: determine which projects qualify, preserve the approved workbook and amendment history, track Notice-of-Intent evidence, monitor the 50% sales threshold, and reconcile outstanding proof-of-sale documents before final benefit approval.

A useful bounded workflow would provide:

1. project-level eligibility and affordability-option gates;
2. immutable workbook/version and submission receipts;
3. unit-level sale-proof completeness tracking;
4. exception queues for missing evidence or mismatched project status; and
5. an approval-ready audit packet for counsel, finance, and HPD submission owners.
