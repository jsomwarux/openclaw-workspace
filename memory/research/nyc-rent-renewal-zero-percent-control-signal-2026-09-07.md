# NYC rent-renewal control signal — 2026-09-07

## Verified signal

- The NYC Rent Guidelines Board adopted a **0% adjustment** for both one- and two-year rent-stabilized apartment renewal leases commencing from **October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027**.
- New York State HCR says NYC owners must deliver written renewal notices **not more than 150 days and not less than 90 days** before the existing lease expires; renewal leases generally retain the same terms and conditions as the expiring lease.

## Operational implication

This is a timely control-record problem for NYC property managers, not a new offer or prospecting lane. Renewal workflows should make the effective guideline period explicit and preserve evidence for:

1. lease-expiration date;
2. 90–150 day offer-window calculation;
3. correct 0% one- or two-year adjustment for qualifying commencement dates;
4. offer generation and delivery timestamp;
5. tenant term selection and returned-document status;
6. exception review before any final send.

The practical angle for an existing warm conversation is: **Can the team prove each renewal used the correct guideline and was offered inside the statutory window without reconstructing the record manually?**
