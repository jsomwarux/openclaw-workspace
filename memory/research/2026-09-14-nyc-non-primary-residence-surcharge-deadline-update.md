# NYC Non-Primary Residence Surcharge — Deadline Update

Date checked: 2026-09-14

## Verified signal

NYC Department of Finance now lists **October 6, 2026** as the exemption-application deadline for both residential homes/condos and cooperative units that received a possible non-primary-residence surcharge notice.

This supersedes the previously published September 18 date. Operational materials, resident notices, and internal trackers should use the live Department of Finance date.

Source: https://home4.nyc.gov/site/finance/property/non-primary-residence-surcharge.page

## Buyer implication

Co-op/condo boards and property managers have a short extension window to:

- identify units that received notices;
- collect each unit's notice PIN/security code and supporting primary-residence documents;
- track submitted, incomplete, rejected, and unresolved applications;
- preserve evidence of resident communications and submission status; and
- escalate exceptions before October 6.

## Workflow opportunity

Offer a lightweight portfolio exception-and-evidence workflow: building/unit roster, owner outreach status, document checklist, deadline countdown, submission receipt, and escalation queue. The value is control and auditability across multiple buildings—not tax advice or exemption eligibility judgment.
