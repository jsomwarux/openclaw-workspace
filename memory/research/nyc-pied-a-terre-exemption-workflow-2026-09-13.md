# NYC Pied-à-Terre Exemption Workflow Signal — 2026-09-13

## Verified signal

NYC extended the exemption-application deadline for the non-primary residence property surcharge to **September 18, 2026**. The city says the extension applies to recipients of a Department of Finance “You may be subject to...” notice and is coordinating with co-op and condominium boards, property managers, and building representatives to reach affected residents.

Source: [NYC Mayor's Office — deadline extension](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/08/mayor-mamdani-and-commissioner-lee-extend-deadline-for-pied-a-te)

## Buyer implication

For co-op/condo managers and boards, the deadline creates a short, portfolio-wide coordination problem:

- resolve the affected-unit roster;
- send resident notices and capture acknowledgements;
- collect primary-residence or other exemption evidence;
- track submission status and exceptions through September 18;
- retain a building-level evidence trail for board and owner follow-up.

## Workflow angle

Offer a bounded **deadline exception-control workflow**: import the notice roster, assign each unit an owner/status, automate reminders, route incomplete evidence to a human reviewer, and produce a final submitted/unresolved proof report by building.

Position around missed-deadline prevention and evidence visibility, not tax advice. A qualified tax or legal professional remains responsible for exemption eligibility.
