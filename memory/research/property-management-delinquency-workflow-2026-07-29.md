# Property Management Delinquency Workflow Signal - 2026-07-29

Source: heartbeat client/market research using local Brave-direct search.

## Signal
- Current rent collection software messaging is converging on integrated payment workflows, PMS/accounting visibility, resident self-service, automated reminders, and exception handling instead of "collections" as a standalone task. Source: RealPage, "Essential Rent Collection Software Tools" (2026-07-25), https://www.realpage.com/blog/essential-rent-collection-software-tools/
- AI property management positioning now explicitly names delinquency-risk spotting, anomaly detection, and portfolio-level financial automation. Source: MRI/RentPayment, "AI Property Management in 2026" (2026-07-07), https://mrisoftware.rentpayment.com/blog/ai-property-management-use-cases-software-options/
- Operator-facing delinquency content is emphasizing proactive communication, consistent systems, earlier risk identification, and operational consistency. Source: PM Assist, "The Collections Conversation Property Managers Need to Start Having Again" (2026-07-05), https://pmassist.substack.com/p/the-collections-conversation-property
- NYC/New York-specific arrears writing reinforces the internal workflow opportunity: identify and stratify arrears trends earlier, prioritize outreach, tailor engagement, track follow-up, and reduce cross-team/platform information chasing. Source: Enterprise Community Partners, "Preserving Stability for Tenants and Housing Providers in New York" (2026-07-21), https://www.enterprisecommunity.org/community-highlights/preserving-stability-tenants-and-housing-providers-new-york
- Legal/compliance-facing NY commentary still makes timing discipline operationally important, including monthly calendars for delinquency notices. Source: WNY Lawyers, "NY 2026 Landlord Legal Compliance Update" (2026-05-26), https://www.wny-lawyers.com/2026/02/ny-2026-landlord-legal-compliance-update/

## JT Relevance
- Altmark rent delinquency proof should stay framed as a control workflow: source/export gate, owner approval, exception queue, notice timing discipline, audit trail, and closeout path.
- Run Control property-management language should lead with "earlier arrears visibility and cleaner follow-up ownership" rather than generic AI automation.
- Useful buyer-facing proof row: delinquency account identified -> source verified -> next action assigned -> communication approved/sent -> result recorded.
