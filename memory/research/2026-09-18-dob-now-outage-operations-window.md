# NYC DOB NOW/BIS outage — property-operations workflow signal

Date checked: 2026-09-18

## Verified signal

NYC Department of Buildings says all DOB NOW modules and the Buildings Information System will be unavailable from **Friday, September 25, 2026 at 5:00 PM through Saturday, September 26, 2026 at 11:00 PM** for an internal-system upgrade.

Source: [NYC DOB Service Updates](https://home4.nyc.gov/site/buildings/dob/service-updates.page)

## Buyer implication

Property managers, filing representatives, and building operators need a pre-outage control window for permits, inspections, compliance filings, license actions, and evidence capture. A lightweight portfolio workflow can:

1. inventory DOB/BIS-dependent actions by building and deadline;
2. require owners to finish or explicitly defer time-sensitive submissions before the outage;
3. preserve confirmation numbers and screenshots before systems go offline;
4. queue post-outage reconciliation and exception checks for September 27.

This is a timely operational-control angle, not a claim that every portfolio has a filing due. No outreach, task creation, or schedule change occurred.
