1. Tricolon method sentences: map/build/measure, file/inbox/approver, dry runs/logs/alerts.
2. Framework announcements: “Three shapes come up again and again,” “Each step ends in something you can look at.”
3. Proof-engine language exposed to buyers: “records the workflow did not write,” “green status,” “human-approved.”
4. Badge-stack eyebrows: price plus proof plus safety in one row.
5. Clever closers: “by design,” “when it drifts,” “so the build is the straightforward part.”
